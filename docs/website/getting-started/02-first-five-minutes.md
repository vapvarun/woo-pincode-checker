# First 5 minutes with the defaults

The plugin works as soon as you activate it. You do not need to add areas first.

## What happens by default

- The checker appears on product pages, above the Add to cart button.
- **Postcodes with no area** is set to **Automatic**. Every postcode is deliverable, on your default delivery days (3 to 5), until you add your first serviceable area.
- Once you add a serviceable area, only the areas you list are deliverable. Everything else is "not delivered".
- Delivery dates show on the product page, next to each shipping option in the cart and checkout, on the order, and in order emails.
- Checkout blocks orders to postcodes you do not deliver to (nothing is blocked while no area exists).

## Check it works

1. Open any physical product on your store.
2. Type a postcode in the checker and click **Check**.
3. You see "Delivery available to ..." and an arrival estimate such as "Arrives Thu, Oct 8 to Fri, Oct 9".

You can also test from the admin. On **Overview**, use **Test a postcode**, pick a country and click **Test**. The result shows what a shopper sees and which area decided it.

## Adjust the basics

1. Go to **Delivery Dates**.
2. Set **Default delivery days (min)** and **Default delivery days (max)**.
3. Set **Same-day cut-off**, **Working days** and **Holidays** to match how you ship.
4. Click **Save changes**.
